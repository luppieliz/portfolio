field notes · singapore · est. observation 2020—

Constantly building & breaking.

A multidisciplinary designer, creator, and storyteller. I like fast proofs-of-concept — the kind that ship in hours, not days. If I think something should exist, I build it: an interface, a website, a tool, a process.

The path here was one question at a time. I started in interior design, learning to shape beautiful spaces. Then: why not build websites for my interiors? So I studied computer science, picking up UI·UX and engineering along the way. Then: what if my websites get hacked? So, cybersecurity. School ended; the building didn't — these days I build, test, and ship security tooling from scratch, and quietly optimise every process I touch.
